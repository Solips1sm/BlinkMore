The product decision

BlinkMore supports comfortable screen habits through natural blink feedback and short voluntary breaks. It can show differences between two calibrated eyelid signals; it cannot establish a disorder or prescribe correction. Dry eye is multifactorial, and clinical diagnosis requires symptoms and signs beyond webcam measurements. The product therefore offers useful observation and practice without a severity score or a promise to cure symptoms. This boundary follows the NEI dry eye overview and the TFOS DEWS III diagnostic report.

Composition and type

Use an 820 × 580 pt default window and a 680 × 520 pt minimum. The default canvas is 32% smaller than 2.2. Today contains one readiness line, the paired motion and recent rate field, one minute of rhythm, and one reset action. Today and an active practice fit without scrolling at the minimum. History, Settings and the measurement inspector may scroll. Navigation is Today, Practice and History. Settings and Pause stay as quiet utility actions; charger policy belongs in Settings and the native menu, never in the header.

Use the house system font and existing type tokens. Most text is 14 px at weight 400. Labels use weight 500, primary numerals use 28 to 30 px at weight 400, and semibold is limited to a short title or selected state where necessary. Use tabular numerals and regular units. Keep the first viewport under 55 visible words. Remove duplicate totals, goal percentages, ring legends, stacked explanations and persistent architecture copy. Definitions appear in an inspector reached from the relevant value.

Custom visual grammar

Replace generic content glyphs with three custom SVG forms: paired apertures for current relative eyelid opening, a bounded observation trace for rhythm, and a horizon for the look away step. Keep the eight circle brand mark. Utility actions can use native symbols or plain text. There is no icon pack dependency. Do not add anatomy, iris, retinal scans or gaze markers to suggest information the camera does not provide.

The paired apertures are schematic, normalized independently to each eye’s reference. For finite normalized opening o, display gap = 24 × clamp(o, 0, 1.5). The drawing does not compare anatomical eye size. Raw contour points and PCA axes remain available only in the inspector. Suppress the live shape during calibration, stale samples or tracking loss; show a neutral outline and a specific status. Never interpolate through an unobserved interval.

The rhythm trace exposes the existing in memory recent bins, not invented individual blink timestamps. Render at most 60 one second bins with observed exposure and event count. Distinguish observed time without an event from missing coverage using a broken or hatched mark. Recent rate remains 60 × detected events / valid tracked seconds, available after 15 tracked seconds. The numeric baseline uses the existing prior six day weighted rate after five tracked minutes; a zero or absent baseline produces no relative percentage. History uses actual seven day aggregate rates, empty insufficient days and tracked time on focus. It carries no more is better target or green health zone.

One material and color logic

Keep the unchanged Anistratenco foundation, surface and control owners. Use one neutral canvas with structural separators instead of a card grid. Native window elevation supplies the dashboard shadow. The browser concept uses the house panel shadow only to represent that outer window. Controls retain their shared control shadow; embedded drawings add no SVG filters, glows or individual shadows. Shadow, hover fill and edge changes settle immediately. Intentional measurement transitions use existing motion tokens and stop under reduced motion.

Blue belongs to identity and the primary action. Violet and teal identify the two visual measurement roles without implying good or bad health. Light and dark use the same semantic roles from the source tokens. Remove the current page wash and per panel color fields. Where the aperture needs depth, one plot local SVG gradient uses fixed user space coordinates, violet at 13% alpha fading to teal at 7% alpha; no moving stops, per card halos or color animation. Preserve a plain fill fallback. Validate text contrast against the composited field before accepting it. Optional Display P3 comes only after sRGB passes.

The paint owner and crop owner stay separate. Keep the outer window shadow unclipped. Clip only the inner artwork, with a radius reduced by the real inset. Every essential SVG stroke and marker stays at least its half stroke plus four viewBox units inside the plot. Use non scaling strokes. Focus outlines, tooltips and native menus escape the plot clip. These decisions follow the existing surface causality owner.

Practice that feels connected

The primary reset lasts about 30 seconds: ten seconds of optional natural blink play, then a 20 second look away prompt. Blink play responds to detected bilateral eyelid events with a short ripple in the aperture drawing. It has no required repetition count, speed target, complete blink grade or symmetry reward. The second step asks for a real distant object, turns the scene into a quiet horizon and needs no attention to the screen. The timer confirms elapsed guidance, not gaze distance or health benefit. Practice also permits starting either step independently.

Start, Pause reset, Resume and End reset remain available by keyboard and mouse. Escape pauses the session and exposes Resume and End. The user can finish without a camera, and lost tracking only disables optional visual feedback. No blink is required to navigate or dismiss anything. A completion message is neutral. Completed reset counts and timed seconds may be saved as daily aggregates; cancelled or interrupted sessions earn nothing and incur no penalty. No per session timestamps, health questionnaire or persistent raw motion data are added.

We do not copy ScreenBlink’s eye rolls, forced closed eye movement prompts, yawn instruction, source text, assets or landmark model. Its inspected revision provides four rotating static cards, Skip and five minute Snooze, rather than a guided session. That behavior is the reference, not the implementation or efficacy evidence. See the pinned exercise renderer and scheduler. No license grant was found in the inspected tree, so the proposal imports none of its files. This follows GitHub’s licensing guidance.

Intermittent prompts and lifecycle

Make scheduled resets opt in, with a 20 minute starter interval and a bounded 5 to 60 minute setting. Count eligible active screen time, independently of camera permission. Pause eligibility during sleep, a locked or inactive session, monitoring pause, or at least 60 seconds of user inactivity. Restart a fresh interval on return. Keep one due state and one deadline. Later replaces that deadline with a five minute retry; Skip starts a fresh interval. Never replay missed breaks or open a focus stealing window over fullscreen work.

A due reset appears in an already visible Today view and in the native menu as Start reset. It waits quietly when the dashboard is closed, with no web view launched by the scheduler. A live fade finishes under ReminderPolicy before presenting the invitation. Starting a voluntary reset clears any cue and suspends automatic fades for its duration. After completion or cancellation, clear the reminder interval and require fresh valid eye observations before reminders resume. Global pause, sleep, lock, dashboard close and AC loss cancel an active reset without recording completion. Manual pause within a reset freezes its remaining monotonic duration.

Keep tracking phase, fade policy, schedule eligibility and session phase as independent values. A small pure Swift exercise policy owns waiting, due, running, paused and completion transitions; ApplicationController owns the clock and delivery. Rust continues to process eyelid geometry only. The web view renders a native snapshot and sends a small validated action enum. Camera failure must not turn elapsed guidance into a failed exercise. Battery still stops the app according to the existing user requirement, without an AC header badge.

Implementation sequence and ownership

First, replace the shell, hierarchy and gauge system in the existing bundled HTML/CSS/JS. Reuse foundations, surface tokens, controls and checkbox markup. Update DashboardWindowController’s size and navigation mapping and preserve native Settings routing. Expose only the bounded in memory recent bins from DailyStatistics. Preserve historical aggregates and saved preferences; retire goal rings and count racing from the interface without deleting user history.

Second, add the pure native exercise policy, its bounded preferences and ApplicationController lifecycle observers. Add a native Start reset menu action while preserving the original eye icon and right click behavior. Add the Practice surface and two custom scenes through the current strict local bridge. DailyRecord may gain optional reset count and timed seconds fields with legacy decode tests. Export and deletion keep their existing guarantees. Camera data stays out of saved exercise records.

Third, validate composition and behavior in the actual WKWebView and native app. Keep capture at 640 × 480, at most 15 inference frames per second, newest frame consumption, stale rejection and bounded UI delivery. Render the dashboard at most five times per second with one update in flight. Run no idle visualization loop, use no SVG blur filters and create no web content process while the dashboard is closed. Measure the package on the target Intel Mac under a documented CPU load before claiming a performance improvement.

Acceptance before replacement

Render Today, Practice and History in light, dark, reduced motion and increased contrast, at default and minimum size. Today and Practice must fit without cropping or scroll, contain no AC badge or ring legend, and stay under the text budget. Normal text must reach 4.5:1 contrast; essential graphical boundaries and focus states must reach 3:1. Verify regular type weights, safe vector bounds and a single material owner. Test loading, calibration, tracking, eyes closed, no face, multiple faces, weak pose, stale frames, pause, denied permission, missing camera and camera error separately.

Deterministic checks cover finite geometry, no invented gap data, insufficient data versus measured zero, legacy history, session pause/resume, one snooze deadline, no backlog and no camera requirement. Actual Mac checks cover keyboard focus, VoiceOver without repeated countdown speech, original eye icon responsiveness, native menu tracking, fade suppression and recovery, hidden dashboard, sleep/wake, lock/unlock and AC disconnect/reconnect. Tests of timing and UI do not establish dry eye efficacy or anatomical blink completeness.

Build and install once after these checks, with the existing rollback backup. Preserve the camera permission boundary and disclose any fresh OS approval requirement. The separate Anistratenco repository stays read only. The current installed app has not been changed by this planning work.

Resolved choices and remaining reference

The synthesis selects a compact optical instrument rather than several agents’ retained goal traces or four destination layouts. Practice is a real guided flow, not a renamed calibration action. The scheduler defaults off, uses one deadline and waits quietly rather than opening ScreenBlink’s topmost popup. Daily completion aggregates support the user’s requested activity history without an individual session ledger. These are explicit product choices where the individual reviews differed.

The Dolphin reference has not been identified in the supplied source or screenshots. Its typography and composition cannot be claimed as matched. The Anistratenco owners remain the working reference until a link or path identifies Dolphin.

These source notes are planning inputs, not implementation approval or runtime validation. The consolidated plan resolves their conflicting recommendations.

Proposal checks

The browser concept uses 41 visible words on Today, 14 px minimum text and weights 400 and 500. Today, Practice and History fit a 680 × 520 px frame in both themes. Measured main text contrast exceeds 7:1 in light appearance, and the essential eye and rhythm strokes exceed 3:1. These are bounded Chromium concept checks, not WebKit, VoiceOver or native application acceptance. Recorded measurements.

Evidence boundaries

The screen break proposal follows NEI guidance. Studies of the exact 20 second break protocol show limited observed symptom change and mixed results. A specific studied blink regimen does not validate a different game or this webcam detector. The plan measures product usability and detection quality separately from health efficacy.
