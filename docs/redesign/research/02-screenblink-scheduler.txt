ScreenBlink implements periodic eye exercise prompts, not a Pomodoro work and break cycle; the pinned tree has no Pomodoro or focus session code. Exercises default on at 20 minutes, with a 5 to 60 minute slider, while the global sound setting defaults off. Electron Store keeps settings, but the one minute polling loop runs independently of blink tracking and camera state. The defaults and control are in [src/app.tsx](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/src/app.tsx#L43-L63) and [src/app.tsx](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/src/app.tsx#L638-L714); persisted main process defaults and preference handlers are in [electron/main.ts](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L119-L141) and [electron/main.ts](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L1516-L1536).

When due, ScreenBlink opens a centered popup for 30 seconds and plays the exercise sound if the global sound preference is on. Skip resets the interval. Snooze schedules a separate five minute timeout but leaves the normal interval running. The popup advances and persists its four item rotation as soon as it opens, before any response. There is no Done action or completion record. See [popup lifecycle](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L1566-L1642), [timer and actions](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L1644-L1700), [exercise rotation](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/public/popup-renderer.js#L33-L75), and [sound playback](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L2065-L2118).

ScreenBlink handles suspend and resume but has no idle or lock and unlock listeners. It resets the exercise timestamp on startup and wake, so persisted timing is discarded at both boundaries; the exercise popup and snooze timeout are not explicitly stopped on suspend. Disabling exercises and enabling them much later can fire immediately from the old timestamp. Resetting preferences stops exercise monitoring, restores it as enabled, and does not restart it. These are behaviors to avoid. See [sleep handlers](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L1716-L1850), [startup](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L1892-L1897), [reset](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L2026-L2063), and [enable, disable, and interval handlers](https://github.com/katunli/ScreenBlink/blob/8b801ce1d43a285938f5fb07ca8bed7a20231080/electron/main.ts#L1516-L1536).

BlinkMore already owns the relevant lifecycle. [BlinkMoreACAgent.swift](<Automation/BlinkMoreACAgent.swift:24>) launches on AC, stops the app on battery, and reconciles after wake; [ApplicationController.swift](<BlinkMore/App/ApplicationController.swift:87>) also checks AC. Its five minute pause is persisted and releases the camera through [ApplicationController.swift](<BlinkMore/App/ApplicationController.swift:93>) and [AppPreferences.swift](<BlinkMore/Services/AppPreferences.swift:55>). Camera authorization is a separate gate in [ApplicationController.swift](<BlinkMore/App/ApplicationController.swift:98>). Keep exercise eligibility independent of camera permission because an exercise needs no camera. Keep the existing fade lifecycle in [ReminderPolicy.swift](<BlinkMore/Core/ReminderPolicy.swift:14>) and [OverlayFade.swift](<BlinkMore/Services/OverlayFade.swift:40>); a due exercise can wait until a fade clears, but it must never reset or replace the fade cue.

For the smallest native scheduler, persist only an enabled flag and interval in UserDefaults, defaulting to enabled and 20 minutes with a 5 to 60 minute range. Keep cadence in memory and start a fresh interval when BlinkMore launches on AC, resumes after sleep or lock, exits pause, or returns from at least 60 seconds of user inactivity. Do not deliver or queue prompts while paused, locked, asleep, idle, or off AC. The scheduler states are disabled, waiting, presenting, and suspended. A due deadline presents one prompt. Done is a self reported acknowledgement without statistics, Skip and a 30 second timeout dismiss and start a fresh interval, and Snooze replaces the sole deadline with one five minute retry. Suspending closes any visible prompt and discards stale deadlines, so resume starts cleanly and cannot replay a backlog. Keep the first version silent and omit Pomodoro sessions, completion statistics, and persisted exercise rotation.

The owner paths are a small pure policy in a new `BlinkMore/Core/ExerciseScheduler.swift`, lifecycle and nonmodal prompt delivery in [ApplicationController.swift](<BlinkMore/App/ApplicationController.swift:28>), persisted settings and snapshot fields in [AppPreferences.swift](<BlinkMore/Services/AppPreferences.swift:3>), and controls using the existing bridge in [index.html](<BlinkMore/Resources/UI/index.html:26>) and [dashboard.js](<BlinkMore/Resources/UI/dashboard.js:39>). Add pure transition checks in a new `Tests/ExerciseSchedulerTests.swift`, following [ReminderTests.swift](<Tests/ReminderTests.swift:3>). Acceptance means exactly one delivery after an eligible interval, one retry after snooze, no prompt while ineligible, and a fresh interval after wake, unlock, pause, idle, and AC relaunch. Camera denial must not request permission again or stop exercise scheduling. A live fade must finish under its own policy without being interrupted by an exercise prompt.
