The optional camera path computes average Eye Aspect Ratio from the two eyes and emits face presence, eye landmarks, baseline, and blink events. This is enough to let a custom SVG eyelid react to relative eye openness and show a brief neutral “blink noticed” status. The available signal does not describe gaze direction or physical viewing distance, so neither should become a score or a claim of successful far focus. When camera feedback is unavailable or disabled, the same session should remain a timed visual guide and say that blink feedback is off. For BlinkMore, use one short soft blink reset: a few naturally paced blink prompts followed by a quiet rest prompt to look away if comfortable. A single inline SVG eye can open and close with the prompt, then follow optional eyelid openness without grades, streaks, or gaze and distance checks. Keep the current snooze idea alongside Start, Pause or Resume, Skip, and Escape. Give the screen reader semantic step text and a polite status announcement only when a step changes or a blink is noticed. Treat the eye drawing as decorative when text supplies the same cue. Under reduced motion, use static open and closed eye states with the same text and controls.
