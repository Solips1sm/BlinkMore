The dark synthetic QA capture is readable, but the first screen gives too many values equal emphasis. A large ring sits beside four 31px stat values, then a full width milestones panel and history charts; the blink total repeats in the ring center and first goal row, while the days row shows 5 / 3 and 167%. Browser default bold on strong elements makes the wordmark, goal totals, stat values and milestone titles compete. The vendored foundations already set a 14px floor, 16px panel titles and 14px supporting and control text, so reduce emphasis in the local dashboard instead of changing the shared foundations.

Keep the system font. Use 20px / 26px at weight 600 for BlinkMore, the foundation panel title token at 16px / 17.5px and weight 600, 14px / 20px at weight 400 for labels and controls, and 14px / 22.5px at weight 400 for supporting text. Set stat values to 28px / 34px at weight 600 and goal values to 16px / 20px at weight 500. Use weight 700 nowhere. Set tabular numerals and align values consistently; keep units inline at 14px regular. Every visible label, unit, state and disclosure summary stays at least 14px.

Make the Activity page the everyday view. Title the ring card Activity goals, keep 1 of 3 goals as quiet 14px text, and use the rows Blinks, Tracked minutes and Active days. Show 240 / 300, 20 / 30 min and 5 days, goal 3 respectively; remove all ring percentages, which become misleading after a goal is met. Remove the duplicate center count and leave the eye glyph decorative. Keep one brief context line, Goals are personal activity targets. Use four compact stat cards with one value and one context line each: Last minute, 14.0 blinks/min, 52s observed; Today average, 12.0 blinks/min, 20 min tracked; Vs personal baseline, +20%, Baseline 10.0 blinks/min; Time since blink, 2.4 s, Next cue in 3.6 s. Keep rates descriptive and avoid judging them as healthy, low or improved for eye health.

Keep the goal card and four stat cards visible before history. Collapse Milestones behind the summary 3 of 4 earned; inside, use First blink, 1,000 blinks, 30 minutes tracked and 7 active days, with an unearned state such as 2 days to go instead of a percentage. Put the weekly chart and closure counts in a History disclosure. In the Geometry inspector, group the equations under a collapsed How we measure blinks disclosure; retain the existing What counts disclosure. Put thresholds and the six day weighted baseline method there, leaving the top level cards with observed time only. At narrower widths keep the 14px context lines visible and let them wrap; the current rule hides stat descriptions below 830px while switching to four cramped columns.
