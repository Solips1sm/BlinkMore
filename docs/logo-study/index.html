Read four independent judgments

Judge 1, brand geometry selected 01b. 01b is my pick because its almond contour reads immediately as an eye, while the larger, evenly spaced circles stay distinct in the 16 pixel sample. Its continuous outline has enough weight beside the rounded lowercase wordmark and comes closest to the supplied reference. This is a subjective visual opinion, not a measure of user preference or medical efficacy. The sheets show 24, 20 and 16 CSS pixel samples in original resolution, but cannot confirm optical results on a physical device or across rasterization pipelines.

Judge 2, native scale selected 12b. 12b is the strongest balance: its almond contour reads immediately as an eye, and its eight-dot iris keeps the clearest link to the reference while preserving open space at 16 px. 01b and 01a are close, while 08a and 13 offer sturdy small marks with more familiar or asymmetric character. These are subjective visual judgments from the supplied 16, 20 and 24 CSS pixel SVG previews. Static screenshots cannot reproduce operating-system menu antialiasing.

Judge 3, type and expression selected 01. This is a subjective visual ranking. 01 wins because its continuous almond silhouette most closely follows the supplied reference, keeps the eight circle iris legible at 16 pixels, and sits naturally beside the Comfortaa wordmark. The sheets show static 24, 20, and 16 CSS pixel samples in light and dark layouts; they do not show the marks in an actual menu bar, on device pixel grids, or at real viewing distances.

Judge 4, visual identity selected 12b. 12b is my strongest choice because its calm almond silhouette reads immediately as an eye, while the centered eight dot iris carries the Eight Circle idea without adding another symbol. The open shape and restrained detail stay balanced beside the Comfortaa wordmark in both themes, including the shown 24, 20 and 16 CSS pixel samples. These stills cannot establish recognition in a real interface, motion behavior or user preference. This is a subjective visual opinion, not measured preference or medical evidence.
