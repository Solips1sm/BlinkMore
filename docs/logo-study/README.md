# Eyesight logo study

The study contains fifteen initial eye concepts and two refinements of every concept, for forty five distinct SVG files. Six designers using gpt-6-luna at max reasoning developed the thirty refinements. Four independent judges using the same model and reasoning level inspected every candidate in six actual WebKit screenshot sheets across light and dark themes. Each judge also inspected the supplied reference. They did not receive the other judges’ verdicts or the designers’ preferences.

The recommended mark is 01b, Full aperture. It ranked first, second, second and third across the four complete rankings, giving it the strongest equal weight mean position of 2.0. Its fuller almond and larger eight circle iris remain clear beside Comfortaa and retain more structure in the small samples. The closest alternative is 12b, Eight circle iris. It received two first place votes and a mean position of 2.75. It offers a quieter, finer contour. Judge 3 selected the original 01. This is a close visual choice between variations of a coherent eye family, not a unanimous vote.

Selection uses the mean position across all four complete rankings, with equal weight for every judge. Lower is stronger. First choice votes break an equal mean, followed by the candidate ID. Every raw ranking and the complete aggregate remain in reviews/ and selection.json. review-snapshot.json records the hashes of the exact SVGs and screenshots assessed. aggregate-reviews.py validates the evidence before recomputing the recommendation.

The fixed blue icon field keeps background color and platform corner treatment constant. The concepts vary the eye’s own rounding, aperture, weight, topology, iris construction and overall logic. Each contact card also shows the actual Comfortaa wordmark and monochrome samples at 24, 20 and 16 CSS pixels. Screenshots use the local WebKit renderer at the host backing scale. They are not captures of a real macOS menu bar.

Open index.html for the interactive gallery, light and dark appearance controls, family filters, individual analyses and SVG downloads. The concepts and refinements folders contain every original vector. recommended/ contains the leading transparent mark, a blue mark, a blue app tile and the nearest alternative. The Comfortaa font and unmodified SIL license are bundled in assets/. The SVG marks contain no text and need no installed font; the gallery wordmark uses the bundled font.

01 Soft almond. A calm, continuous almond with softened joins. The original eight dot iris remains the clearest link to the company. The closest evolution of the supplied mark. Fine dots need a small size check.

01a Quiet almond. A flatter upper and lower curve gives the eight circle iris more room while keeping the original almond calm. The low profile feels relaxed, though the outline takes less height in a menu bar.

01b Full aperture. A fuller almond and larger iris use more of the canvas and keep the eight circles distinct at small sizes. The taller gaze feels more alert and may carry less of the original mark’s quiet mood.

02 Pill lens. A capsule turns the familiar button pill into an eye. Straight upper and lower spans make the mark compact and steady. Strong family resemblance to the controls. Can read as a switch without the iris.

02a Bold capsule. A thicker capsule outline and a wider iris make the button pill read as an eye at menu size. The stronger border can still suggest a control when shown without the wordmark.

02b Aperture capsule. A transparent almond cut through a solid capsule turns the pill into a lens while retaining the circle motif. The dense silhouette depends on the background showing clearly through the aperture.

03 Soft rectangle. A continuous rounded enclosure gives the eye a device like frame while retaining a living iris. More architectural than organic. Avoid letting the outline resemble a camera housing.

03a Lidded frame. Shallow inward bends along the frame’s top and bottom edges give the rounded rectangle a softer eye shape. The bent edges make the frame less literal as a device window.

03b Bold frame. A heavier soft rectangle and larger radial iris keep the eight circle motif centered and defined at small sizes. The horizontal sides can still read as a screen around the eye.

04 Open lids. Two separate arcs leave the corners open, suggesting a relaxed blink rather than an enclosing shield. Airy and responsive. Open corners may weaken the silhouette at small sizes.

04a Open lids, calm aperture. The rounded upper arc and closer lower lid make the two open strokes more deliberate while the tighter eight circle iris retains the company motif. The heavier lid reads clearly at small sizes, though the separated corners still depend on a crisp silhouette.

04b Open lids, relaxed slit. A shallow upper lid and a gently rising lower curve create a calm narrow aperture with the iris visibly centered between them. The open corners stay expressive, while the smaller aperture places the iris closer to the upper lid.

05 Sculpted lens. A heavier upper lid and a quiet lower curve create a filled, sculptural eye with a clear aperture. A stronger app icon shape. The weighted upper lid needs balance beside Comfortaa.

05a Sculpted lens, tapered lid. The filled upper lid tapers from a broad center into fine corners, leaving a spacious lower aperture and an intact eight circle iris. The wide negative space feels light, while the narrower lid carries less app icon weight.

05b Sculpted lens, bold lid. A taller filled canopy and deeper lower curve turn the eye into a more sculptural mark while keeping the pupil and eight circles legible. The strong upper mass holds at menu size, though it may feel heavier beside Comfortaa.

06 Ellipse orbit. An elliptical eye emphasizes continuous observation and a generous aperture without sharp corners. Soft and simple, with less explicit eye character than the almond.

06a Soft capsule orbit. Flatter crown and base curves evolve the ellipse into a soft capsule orbit, preserving continuous enclosure around the eight circle iris. The compact contour is steady at small sizes, with less natural almond character than a curved eye.

06b Open orbit. A small opening on the right turns the closed ellipse into an orbit path that suggests observation in motion while retaining the central motif. The gap adds direction, though it can read as a tracking symbol at a glance.

07 Lens brackets. Two curved brackets frame the iris. The missing top and bottom strokes make the eye feel open and light. Distinct and spacious. Brackets can imply scanning rather than comfort.

07a Soft parenthesis. Thicker rounded parentheses give the open lens brackets a friendlier silhouette while the eight-dot iris stays centered and clear. The compact iris detail may still soften at 16 px.

07b Four soft corners. Splitting each bracket into upper and lower arcs adds breathing room around the same eight-dot iris and makes the gaze feel lighter. The extra gaps weaken the eye outline at very small sizes.

08 Resting blink. A shallow eye and low iris describe a relaxed eyelid with less vertical occupation. Efficient in a menu bar. The compressed eye can look sleepy.

08a Resting capsule. A flatter, rounder shell keeps the shallow eye compact while a low eight-dot iris preserves the resting gaze. The capsule silhouette can read as a control without the iris.

08b Resting open lids. Separate shallow lid arcs leave the corners open and keep the lowered eight-dot iris visible in a calm, horizontal mark. The open corners may close up visually at 16 px.

09 Paired gaze. Two separate eyes distribute the company’s eight circles equally across both sides, making bilateral movement part of the identity. Directly expresses two eyes. Both small irises compete for limited menu bar pixels.

09a Paired pearls. Rounder paired eyes hold four iris dots and a small pupil each, splitting the eight-circle motif evenly across both sides. The two pupils add detail to an already dense small mark.

09b Converging pair. A larger iris group in each rounded eye turns the paired gaze gently inward while preserving four company dots per side. The shared inward direction can make the pair feel more intent than relaxed.

10 Living seed. An asymmetric upper lid suggests movement while the iris holds a stable central anchor. Memorable organic tension. It must remain neutral rather than suggest unequal eye health.

10a Seed sweep. The asymmetric seed becomes two rounded lid strokes, with a heavier upper sweep and the eight circle iris held at center. Open terminals feel light and comfortable, though the isolated arcs rely on their separation to read as an eye at menu size.

10b Seed cutout. A solid asymmetric seed uses an eye shaped negative space for its aperture, giving the organic concept a compact filled silhouette. The stronger body holds up when small, though it has more visual weight beside Comfortaa.

11 Round aperture. A circular outer frame contains a simple eye cut and the eight circle iris, joining observation with a focus dial. Robust circular icon, with a risk of unnecessary nested boundaries.

11a Aperture disc. The circular frame becomes a filled disc with an eye shaped opening, leaving the eight circle iris floating cleanly in the aperture. The simple outer silhouette suits an app mark, though the negative opening needs contrast from its background.

11b Orbit iris. Eight circles move from the iris to a rounded perimeter dial while a simple stroked eye keeps the gaze explicit. The perimeter carries the company motif clearly, though its dotted circle feels more like a focus dial than a continuous frame.

12 Petal iris. Eight rounded radial strokes reinterpret the company’s circles as a soft iris inside the eye. More legible strokes at small sizes. It can resemble a loading spinner if animated continuously.

12a Capsule bloom. Eight short rounded capsules turn the radial strokes into soft petals while keeping the pupil and almond immediately legible. The repeated petals may feel more floral or animated than a quiet iris.

12b Eight circle iris. Eight separate circles make the company motif explicit and keep the iris construction simple at small sizes. The dot ring is less distinctive than radial petals and can read as an aperture.

13 Forward gaze. The iris moves gently to one side inside a generous almond, suggesting attention shifting beyond the screen. A clear eye with directional character. The resting logo should not appear to watch a particular person.

13a Soft directional almond. A rounder right lid gives the almond a gentle forward lean, with the eight circle iris shifted just enough to suggest attention moving outward. The softened right corner reduces the crispness of the eye silhouette.

13b Cutout gaze. A solid almond rim carved around a clear aperture makes a bolder app mark while the offset eight circle iris preserves the forward gaze. The heavier rim can dominate the small iris beside Comfortaa.

14 Ribbon fold. A pair of offset lid ribbons shares rounded endings with the pill controls. The iris sits in their open overlap. Dynamic without arrows. Heavy terminals can crowd the iris.

14a Almond Ribbon. The steeper opposing arcs restore a readable eye silhouette while keeping eight iris dots open between the rounded offset ends. The fuller almond tension feels more expressive and less like a pill control.

14b Pill Fold. Flatter curves carry the pill control language into both lids around a centered eight dot iris. The shallow upper lid leaves less clearance above the iris and can suggest a switch.

15 Constellation eye. Eight dots form the eye boundary itself, letting the company motif become the whole silhouette. A pupil makes the eye reading explicit. The strongest departure from a conventional eye icon. Sparse dots need careful spacing at 16 pixels.

15a Even Constellation. Eight equally weighted circles form the eye boundary while a larger center dot establishes the pupil. Even spacing gives the small mark rhythm but leaves larger gaps in the eye contour.

15b Anchored Constellation. Larger corner circles anchor the eye while six smaller arc points retain the eight circle company motif. Varied circle sizes soften the rhythm and make the boundary more decorative.

The recommendation is a set of subjective model visual judgments. It does not establish human preference or physical device legibility. The recommended mark has not replaced the installed app identity. No application branding is changed by this study.
